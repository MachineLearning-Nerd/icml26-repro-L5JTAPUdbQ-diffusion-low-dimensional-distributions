# Claim 4 threshold-falsification method

Use the exact Claim 1 continuous-time counterexample, not the rejected Euler
diagnostic. The target `Uniform{-1,+1}` is atomic and has neither ambient nor
intrinsic Lebesgue density. Its support is nonconvex, so it is not a
log-concave measure. Consequently, Holder density or score regularity, uniform
density bounds, and a positive density lower bound are all unavailable.

The target nevertheless satisfies both paper assumptions and has trivial exact
subspace recovery. The paper's density threshold creates a positive-probability
zero-score tail event. The reverse drift then amplifies the output enough to
contradict Theorem 2's Wasserstein rate for every fixed polylogarithmic factor.
Because one of the claimed results fails inside the exact weak-regularity
domain, the broad Claim 4 statement is false as stated.
