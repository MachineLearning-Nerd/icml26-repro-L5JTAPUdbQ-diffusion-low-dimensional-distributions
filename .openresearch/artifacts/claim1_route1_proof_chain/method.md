# Claim 1 route 1 method

1. Hash the pinned paper source and inspect the theorem and proof markers.
2. Pin the external Wasserstein-from-score inequality and its provenance.
3. Starting from the `n^-1/2` square-root score term, derive the geometric-time
   sum symbolically for every `k=1,...,8`.
4. Substitute the paper's `tau=n^(-2/k)` only after deriving the independent
   time singularity. This yields `n^-1/2` for `k<=2` and `n^-1/k` for `k>2`.
5. Trace `sqrt(d)*sqrt(d M^3/n)=d M^(3/2)n^-1/2`.
6. Verify whether the event used to make this chain unconditional is justified.
7. Mutate the committed `tau` exponent; the independent checker must exit
   nonzero.

No sample size, tolerance, or experimental horizon is selected from a desired
first hit. This route is a proof dependency certificate, not a finite scaling
fit.
