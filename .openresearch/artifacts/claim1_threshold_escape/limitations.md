- The numerical Euler attempt is retained only as a rejected diagnostic; the
  verdict rests on the continuous-time event certificate.
- The asymptotic contradiction treats `polylog n` in its standard meaning: a
  fixed finite power of `log n` with an `n`-independent constant.
- The proof applies to the paper's thresholded estimator. Disabling the
  density threshold invalidates this specific escape event.

  diffusion estimator fails on the Rademacher target.
