# Claim 2 numerical-route limitations

This finite study is scoped corroboration, not proof of a universal upper
bound. It covers the component-estimation mechanism at `M=1`, where mixture
weights are trivial. Its purpose is to independently test the executable
estimator, `N` scaling, uncertainty, and the predicted removal of ambient
normal-coordinate estimation error. The proof certificate separately audits
the mixture-weight and aggregation dependency chain.
