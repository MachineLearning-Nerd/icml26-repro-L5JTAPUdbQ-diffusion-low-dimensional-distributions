# Claim 4 limitations

This is a full-paper-scale target and a direct test of a distribution with no
density, but it remains a finite experiment at one diffusion time. It directly
corroborates the score-estimation result conditional on known subspaces; it
does not by itself establish the universal theorem or repair the invalid
unconditional recovery lemma identified by Claim 3.

The regularization-set constant is not numerically specified in the paper.
This experiment precommits `C_R=4` and reports the exclusion fraction. That
implementation choice is a material but transparent deviation and limits the
final confidence to MEDIUM.
