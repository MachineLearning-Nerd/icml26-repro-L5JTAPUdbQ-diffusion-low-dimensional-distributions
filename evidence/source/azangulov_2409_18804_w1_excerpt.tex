The score matching loss controls~\cite[Eq. (90)]{oko2023} the Wasserstein loss between the target $\mu$ and the approximation $\hat{\mu}_{\underline{T}}$.
\label{thm:from_score_matching_to_wasserstein}
W_1\del{\hat{\mu}, \mu} \lesssim \sqrt{D}\del{\sqrt{\underline{T}} + \sum_{k=0}^{K-1} \sqrt{\log \delta^{-1}\cdot\sigma^2_{T_{k+1}} \int_{T_k}^{T_{k+1}} \expectation\|\hat{s}\del{t,X(t)}-s\del{t,X(t)}\|^2 dt} + \delta + e^{-\overline{T}}}.
Eq. (90) in \cite{oko2023} does not address the dependence on the ambient dimension $D$; the authors state that it follows from Lemma D.7.
